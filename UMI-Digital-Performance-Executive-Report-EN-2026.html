UMI Lead Fourm generated the 82 lead results, with $819.95 spend: approximately $10.00 per lead. Dividing all account spend by 82 gives $33.26, but mixes different campaign objectives and is not the lead campaign CPL.

Attribution and non-additivity

A consistent structure for every service page

Start with what the service is, where it is delivered and who it serves. Follow with real projects, scope, process, buying considerations, evidence and a relevant enquiry route. Costs, delivery times, awards and client claims need confirmation before publication.

Arabic expansion

Start with genuinely equivalent Arabic pages for exhibitions, kiosks and activation. Use Arabic language and direction settings, with reciprocal hreflang between real language equivalents. Avoid near-identical city or keyword pages.
